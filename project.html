Semester project

Course›Project

Teams of three or four choose one of two systems in week 1 and develop it throughout the semester: conventionally in Block I, with AI agents from week 4. The exercises are written against your project's next feature, so the choice does not change what is asked.

Option A: Smart campus assistant

Study-room booking, events, and student requests routed to the office that can answer them.

Option B: Sustainable mobility planner

Multimodal journeys around the city, across bus, bike and the semester ticket, with a comparison of the options.

Deliverable

A working system and documented engineering evidence: a specification with acceptance criteria, a test adequacy argument, a review and evaluation record, an accountability statement, and an AI-use log.

Use whatever tools you like. Document what you used. Never submit anything you cannot explain.
